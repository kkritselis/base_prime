    How to measure it

      What this plan doesn't know yet

      • Cost and conversion: Base's cost per lead, close rate and pricing by channel. With them, effort shares become a budget.
      • Competition: where generator and solar-plus-battery installers already advertise.
      • Existing customers: ZIPs where Base already has homes (referral and neighbor campaigns work best there).
      • Street-level outages: outage history is by county; Base's own battery fleet would show outages street by street.
